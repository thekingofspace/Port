About

What I work in, what I have shipped, and how I like to build.

I started writing Luau in 2019 and Rust in 2022. These days my games are written in TypeScript and lean heavily on classes, with the GUI built in Vide, though the Luau underneath is still where I do my deepest work.

Most of what I enjoy sits below the gameplay layer, in the systems everything else stands on. When a project needs networking, serialization, input handling or logging, I would rather write the library properly once than patch around the same gap in every feature that follows. Pigeon, Buffer, LehuaClass, InputController, ReplicationPP and Blackbox all came out of exactly that instinct, and so did SCP CoreLib on the Minecraft side.

Lehua is the largest of them. It is a Luau runtime written in Rust on top of mlua and tokio that runs a project immediately and then builds it into one standalone binary, with a standard library covering the filesystem, networking, databases, cryptography, regex, processes and parallel virtual machines.

I also model, draw and design GUI, which means I can take a creature from a sketch to a rigged model to the code that makes it fight you, then build the screen it appears on. Advanced on the front end and on the back end, and comfortable on either side of the wire.
