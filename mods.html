Why a library first

The same habit, in a different engine

CoreLib exists for the same reason Pigeon and Blackbox exist. Two mods needed the same containment plumbing, so it got written once, properly, and both mods became a thin layer of the parts that are actually interesting. The engine changes, the instinct does not.
